# Shop art

Issue #18 · branch `shop-art`

## What

Give each shop a silhouette that reads at walking zoom. The Campus Café has a round cup sign and a terracotta counter. The Merchandise shop frames a navy hoodie. Three upright boards mark the teal Surf Shop, and a coral-and-cream awning shades the Beach Café.

Each place has a sign with its name. A locked place has a shutter and displays its unlock price. An unlocked place shows its level, including purchases of staff places. Its fittings follow the products in the latest shop response after purchases, reloads, demo loads and resets.

| Shop | Product fittings |
| --- | --- |
| Cafe | Espresso machine, cake under glass, basket of bagels |
| Merchandise | Framed hoodie, folded shirts, cap display |
| Surf Shop | Rental boards, wetsuit rail, wave lesson board |
| Beach Cafe | Beer crate, drinks fridge, wrapped sandwich case |

## Decide

Draw at the existing furniture and pavilion footprints. Keep fixed furniture visible when the product on it is unavailable. Use the server's product IDs to choose fittings, since two shops at the same level can own different upgrades. Use text nodes for price and level signs. The Nova roof controls visibility of the indoor signs.

The silhouette reference is Felix Antes’ [Nova Beach Ventures](https://github.com/felix-anthes-png/nova-beach-ventures/blob/main/specs/store-building-specialization.md). The new fittings are original Canvas drawings based on the group's coastal concept sheet.

## Out of scope

Trading rules, unlock mechanics, staff behaviour, walking paths, campus architecture and landscaping.
